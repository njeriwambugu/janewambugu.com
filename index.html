About Me

I'm a software developer and designer focused on building robust, intuitive digital experiences. My work brings together thoughtful engineering and clear, purposeful visual design.

I value maintainable code, accessible interfaces, strong visual hierarchy, and motion that supports the experience rather than distracting from it. Each project is an opportunity to solve a real problem with care and precision.

I continue to grow through hands-on project work, open-source contribution, technical writing, and mentorship.

My Approach

I start by understanding the goal and who it’s for.

  1. 01

    Discover

    I learn what the project needs and who it’s for.

  2. 02

    Define

    I agree on the goal and what a good result looks like.

  3. 03

    Design

    I shape the layout and visual direction.

  4. 04

    Animate

    I add movement when it helps the idea come across.

  5. 05

    Refine

    I test the details and polish the finished work.
